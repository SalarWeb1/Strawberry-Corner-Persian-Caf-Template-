# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to Strawberry Corner to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion or sexual identity.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour**
- Being kind and patient in reviews and issues
- Giving constructive, specific feedback
- Respecting differing viewpoints and design opinions
- Crediting others' work

**Unacceptable behaviour**
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Spam, or self-promotion unrelated to the project

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via the contact form at [salarweb.ir](https://salarweb.ir). Reports are reviewed and answered within 72 hours. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block contributors who violate this code.
